AI principles in Wisal

AI is an optional tool, not an open-ended promise.

العربية

1) Optional and off by default

Hosted generation is off on every new install and every upgrade. You turn it on after checking your Groq account's plan, model permissions and usage limits. Without it, Wisal works with local templates that are labeled as templates.

2) Not "free and unlimited"

Generation uses the Groq provider and open-weight GPT OSS models. A model being open-weight does not make its hosted service free or unlimited for your account. Every request counts against your own account's quota.

  • No automatic switch to a paid plan, another provider or a local server.
  • Retired models are not silently replaced: you pick a supported model yourself.
  • Exhausted quota, rate limits, key problems and network failures each get a clear message.

3) What is sent to the provider

With generation enabled, a request may include the name, relationship, notes, situation, draft and the style examples you chose. The app tells you this before you enable generation. Avoid writing sensitive information.

Local storage does not mean local processing: with generation enabled, the needed text goes to Groq.

4) Clear limits on every request

  • Up to 16,000 input characters and 1,024 tokens for the reply.
  • 45 seconds per request at most, two attempts at most, and retries only on rate limits or server errors.
  • An empty or malformed reply is treated as a failure, never shown as an AI result.

These limits are safeguards, not a cost guarantee or an exact quota calculation.

5) The AI does not act on your behalf

  • The model only writes text. It has no access to contacts, messages, files or sending.
  • Model instructions forbid inferring sensitive personal traits and inventing promises. Those instructions do not replace your review.
  • The optional automated review is not a guarantee of correctness. Your own review before sending is what counts.

6) Languages, honestly

The focused Android composer supports Arabic and English, keeping your Arabic dialect choice. The Spanish interface is a beta translation and is not evidence of Spanish message quality. Other languages and real model quality have not been verified.
