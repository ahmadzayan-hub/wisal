Wisal for business

Professional replies to customers, one message with your review each time.

العربية

Wisal business mode helps you reply to customers with a professional, personal message, one message with your review each time. It is separate from the family space, and you turn it on yourself.

1) Available today

  • Business mode lives inside the Broadcast screen and is turned on by an explicit choice.
  • A personal reply in a professional tone for customers who already have an open chat with you.
  • It opens WhatsApp Business with the message ready. If Business is not installed, it opens regular WhatsApp and tells you.
  • One tap per customer. There is no "send to all" button.
  • Multiple sender accounts: each has a name, a default country code, a signature and a channel (regular WhatsApp or Business).
  • Instagram, Messenger and LinkedIn: Wisal prepares and copies the message and opens the app; you paste and send.

2) Why there is no automatic bulk sending

WhatsApp bans numbers that send bulk messages to people who did not start a conversation. A personalised message with one tap per customer is slower, but it does not put your business number at risk of a ban. Even with manual taps, avoid sending the same text to dozens of people quickly.

3) Legitimate automated sending (optional, needs your setup)

The official route is the WhatsApp Business Cloud API from Meta. It needs:

  • A verified Meta Business account and a number dedicated to the API, not your personal number.
  • Message templates approved by Meta for messages outside the 24-hour window.
  • A server that keeps the access token safe. Free-form replies are allowed only within 24 hours of the customer's last message.

Wisal includes an optional server for this, with one button per message, and it works only after explicit setup with your account. If you enable it, messages pass through the service provider you configure, and we tell you so before activation.

4) Not built yet

  • Customer consent records (source, date, purpose and status).
  • Consent-gated sending, an audit trail and opt-out.

This is a later phase. Until it is built, you are responsible for your customers' consent to be contacted.

5) AI in business mode

Replies are generated like the rest of Wisal: Groq is optional, and the needed context text is sent to it. Generation requests to Groq are not end-to-end encrypted; the provider sees the text needed for generation. Do not put sensitive customer data in the context.
