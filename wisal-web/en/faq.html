Frequently asked questions

Short answers taken from the current Wisal documentation.

العربية

Use and cost

Is Wisal free?

Local templates work without an account or a network. Groq is optional and has quotas and terms that depend on your account plan. There is no promise of unlimited free use, and no automatic switch to a paid plan.

Which devices does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later, and Windows 10 or later.

Do I need the internet?

No. If cloud AI is off, there is no connection, or the quota has run out, Wisal shows local templates in Arabic and English, clearly labelled as templates.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. It opens the person's chat with the message ready, and you tap Send yourself.

Messages and AI

Does Wisal send messages on its own?

No. Wisal suggests, you review and edit, and the final tap is always yours. Group sending happens one by one, each with your tap; there is no automatic or scheduled sending.

What is sent to the AI provider?

When cloud generation is on, only the context text the message needs is sent: the relationship type, the name or nickname, your notes, the occasion and your saved style examples. WhatsApp numbers and contacts are not sent. Cloud generation is off by default.

Does the extra review guarantee the message is right?

No. Automated review is not a guarantee of correctness: one model reviewing another can repeat the same mistake. Read the message yourself before you send it.

Data and security

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Wisal has no servers that collect or store your data. If you enable Groq, the request reaches the provider as described above.

How is the Groq key stored?

On Android: in encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore, and it is not included in backups. On Windows: settings, including the key, are in a local JSON file that is not encrypted, so rely on your device's protection and do not share the settings folder.

What does a backup contain?

It does not contain the Groq key, but it does contain your private data: people, history and preferences. Sharing it shares that data, and you are responsible for where you keep it.

How do I update without losing my data?

Take a backup first. An Android update must use the same signing certificate and a higher version number. Do not uninstall the app to get around a signature mismatch: uninstalling can erase your data.

Does Wisal have encrypted direct chats?

Not yet. Wisal Direct is still in development, is not encrypted messaging yet, and is not available in the app as a finished feature. Details and release conditions are on the Security page.

I found a security problem. What should I do?

Send the details to ahmad.zayan@gmail.com, without anyone's real data.
