How Wisal works

Simple steps, and the final decision is always yours.

العربية

Wisal helps you decide what to say, not who someone is. Write to family, friends, colleagues and customers, and review every word before it leaves the app.

1) Choose the person and the purpose

Pick the person you are writing to and say in one line what you want to get across: congratulations, an apology, a request, a follow-up or anything else.

2) Language and tone (optional)

You can choose Arabic or English, the dialect and the tone. If you leave them, Wisal uses your saved settings.

3) Two drafts to compare

Two short suggestions appear side by side. You can edit either one freely.

  • With hosted AI enabled: suggestions come from the Groq provider. Hosted generation is off by default; you turn it on after checking your account's plan.
  • With it off, offline, or out of quota: Wisal shows local Arabic and English templates, clearly labeled as templates, not AI.

4) Extra review (optional)

In an optional mode, a second role checks both drafts against what you wrote: meaning, negation, numbers, timing and promises. A rejected draft gets one repair only, then is reviewed again. Review currently supports Arabic and English only.

Automated review is not a guarantee of correctness: one model reviewing another can repeat the same mistake. Limits: at most four model calls, one repair and 90 seconds for the whole step, and each extra call counts against your account quota. Any edit you make clears the "reviewed" status.

5) You send it

Wisal never sends a message on its own. You copy it, share it, or open WhatsApp with the message ready, and the final tap is yours. Opening another app does not mean the message was sent.

Your preferences, under your control

  • You can edit your communication tone, inspect the style rules, turn them off, or reset what the app has learned.
  • Wisal does not infer sensitive personal traits from a name or a relationship.
