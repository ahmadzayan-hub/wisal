Wisal privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

العربية

This page is an English translation of the Arabic privacy policy. If the two differ, the Arabic text applies.

Wisal is an Android app that helps you draft messages for family, friends, colleagues and customers. It is built on a simple principle: your data is local by default, cloud generation is optional, and sending to your contacts is your decision. This page explains clearly what is stored, where, and why.

1) Local storage on Android

  • The people you add (name or nickname, relationship, an optional WhatsApp number, notes, occasions, a photo you choose from your gallery).
  • Your settings (tone, dialect, timings), your style preferences and the examples you choose to save, and your message history and favourites.
  • A new Groq key is saved encrypted through Android Keystore. Older keys are migrated only after the encrypted save succeeds.

All of this is stored locally in your app. Wisal has no servers that collect or store your data.

2) The AI provider (Groq)

After you enable Groq and the account plan is checked, whenever you ask for a message or an analysis, the context text needed for generation (such as the relationship type, the name or nickname, the notes you wrote, the occasion and your saved style examples) is sent to the AI provider Groq so it can return a suggestion. We send only what the message needs; no WhatsApp numbers and none of your contacts are sent. You can review Groq's privacy policy.

Cloud generation is off by default. Local templates are clearly marked and do not send any request to Groq. Background cloud suggestions are a separate choice and use your account's quota.

3) Permissions and why we ask for them

  • Internet: for cloud generation and the integrations you choose to enable.
  • Contacts (optional): to import birthdays or prepare a group message with each person's name. Reading happens on your device, and your contacts are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Calendar (optional): to suggest an occasion from your agenda. Reading is local.
  • Notifications: to remind you of occasions and suggestion times.

4) Sending (WhatsApp and others)

Wisal does not send any message automatically. When you choose "Send", the app opens WhatsApp with the message ready, and the final tap on "Send" is yours. Group sending happens one by one, each with your tap; there is no automatic blast.

5) Children

The app is intended for adults. Do not add information about children to context that is sent to an external provider.

6) Controlling and deleting your data

  • You can edit or delete people and messages, and review or clear style preferences.
  • Do not uninstall the app to get past an update signature mismatch. Export a backup first, and check that the version number and signature match.
  • The backup you export yourself does not contain the Groq key, and you are responsible for where you keep it.

7) Contact

For any privacy question: ahmad.zayan@gmail.com

We may update this policy when we add new features, and we will update the "Last updated" date above. This page describes the current source version; an older installed version may differ.
