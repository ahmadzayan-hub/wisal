العربية

A drafting assistant for family, friends, colleagues and customers that helps you say what you mean, in your own style. AI helps you express yourself; it never speaks for you.

Get Wisal for Android Windows version

Android 8.0 or later, ARM64. Check the release version and its signature before updating, and do not uninstall your app to get around a signature conflict.

Why Wisal

  • AI that helps you express, not speak for you: two suggestions in the tone of each relationship, which you review and edit before sending. No automatic sending, no scheduling, no imitating your voice.
  • For the people in your life: family, friends, colleagues and customers. Tone is a preference you choose and edit, not an analysis of anyone's personality.
  • Languages: messages today are in Arabic and English, with Arabic dialect options. We do not claim every language is tested or that every model's quality is guaranteed.
  • Linked to your calendar: reads your device's occasions locally and prepares a fitting message ahead of time.
  • A separate business mode: professional replies to customers, apart from the family space, with clear consent and one reviewed message each time. See Wisal for business.

Three steps

  1. Download and open. Add the people you want to reach: a name and a relationship are enough. No account needed.
  2. Ask for a suggestion. Two suggestions in that person's tone and language; pick one and edit it.
  3. Send it yourself. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. The final tap is always yours.

Privacy, stated plainly

  • Personal assistant mode: drafting data is stored locally. When Groq is enabled, a request may include the name, relationship, notes, context and your style examples. Local templates need no provider.
  • Sending is always yours: no automatic sending, no scheduled sending, no automatic replies in your name. Every message is an editable draft until you tap Send.
  • Business mode (optional and separate): if you enable it, sending through the WhatsApp Business API goes through the service provider you configure. We say so before activation, and there is never automatic bulk sending.

No ads and no selling of data. Details in the privacy policy.
